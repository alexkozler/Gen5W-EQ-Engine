EQ Engine rescue stick
======================

Keep this on its own USB stick, in the glovebox. Don't leave it plugged in.

What it does
Turns EQ Engine off: later starts skip EQ Engine's boot step, the boot step is
stopped, the in-memory change to Android's effects list is removed, and
Android's audio converter setting goes back to its default (this also turns
off the very high quality converter test). It changes nothing else. A report
goes to EQENGINE-RESCUE/ on this stick.

When to use it
Only if something is wrong and you can't reach the EQ Engine app or NEMESIS
Manager, for example if sound or the screen misbehaves after installing EQ
Engine.

How
- Android running (screen works): plug the stick in and answer Yes.
- Android not coming up: plug the stick in. NEMESIS runs it without asking,
  because the file NEMESIS.emergency is on this stick.
Then restart the head unit by switching the car off and on again. EQ Engine
stays off until you turn it back on in its app, or uninstall it in NEMESIS
Manager > My Packages.
